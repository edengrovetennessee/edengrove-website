The Development

What Laurelbrooke Is

01

The Setting

578 acres off Sneed Road, between Hillsboro Road and Old Hillsboro Road in Franklin’s Grassland area — 200 of them preserved woodland. Two entrances, both with guard posts staffed around the clock: not cameras, not a keypad, but people. Very little else in Franklin is gated this way.

Southern Land Company; RealTracs MLS via nashvillehome.guru

02

The Community

311 homesites, begun by Southern Land Company in 1999 and long since built out, arranged in five distinct neighbourhoods — the Woodlands, the Estates, the Reserve, the Lakes and the Boulevard. Private roads, stone walls, creeks and two stocked ponds.

Southern Land Company; RealTracs MLS via nashvillehome.guru

03

The Homes

French Chateau, Tudor and Traditional, with all-brick or brick-and-stone exteriors as standard. Houses sold in the past year ran 4,297 to 14,562 square feet, median 7,158, built between 2000 and 2020 on lots from half an acre to three. Builders included Ford Custom Classic Homes, Legend Homes, Davis Properties, Hancock Construction and Classic Design Homes.

RealTracs MLS via nashvillehome.guru, twelve months to August 2026

Renovation Is the Market

Five of the eight published closings carry renovation language in their listings — “extensively and thoughtfully renovated,” “exquisitely redesigned.” The lowest tier, four sales between $1,750,000 and $2,490,000, is specifically the unrenovated 2000 to 2004 stock. That gap is the opportunity.

A $500 Spread Per Foot

Prices ran from $312 to $804 a foot in the same community in the same year. Nowhere else in Franklin is the range that wide, and it is almost entirely a function of whether a house has been brought up to date.

The Record

1805 Pace Haven closed at $11,710,000 — 14,562 square feet on 2.56 acres, six bedrooms and eleven baths, built 2015. It took 168 days and 95.8% of a $12,225,000 ask. It is the highest price the community has recorded.

Availability

Getting In

Built Out, No New Supply

All 311 homesites are long since built. There is no new construction to buy, and no developer left to buy from.

Four and a Half Houses in a Hundred

Fourteen of 311 homes traded in the past year — about 4.5%, among the tightest of any community we cover. Median time to contract was 38 days, but the range ran from one day to 210.

Six on the Market Now

Asking prices run $2,399,000 to $8,500,000, at a $3,199,500 median. Inventory turns over quickly here, so treat any published list as a snapshot.

Behind the Gate

Amenities

A Four-Thousand-Foot Clubhouse

Bookable for private functions, with valet available for larger events, alongside a resort-style aquatic centre.

Courts and Fields

Two lighted tennis courts, pickleball courts, a basketball court, an athletic field, a full fitness centre and a playground.

Woodland and Water

Two hundred acres of preserved woodland, two stocked fishing ponds, and walking trails connecting all five neighbourhoods.

Dues run $4,168 to $5,736 a year, median $5,508, most sections billed quarterly. They cover the round-the-clock staffed security, the clubhouse and aquatic centre, the courts and fields, the ponds and trails, the private roads and the common grounds.
