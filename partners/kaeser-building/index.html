What It Is For

Best case, it sells
before it is finished.

Our objective is always to pre-sell at the highest number an early buyer will pay. But a house like this has to be marketed properly whether or not that happens — because the second thing it is buying you is a reputation as a premier builder in Nashville, and that is worth something on the next one.

Whether it sells in a day or in ninety

By the time the house is finished we have four editorial films, a rendering film, a curated event, a full photographic set and a year of campaign behind it. That is enough to keep it in front of every buyer in Nashville and across the country for as long as it takes — and enough to carry the next one.

Your agent is in front of people, not paperwork

A dedicated transaction coordinator and a dedicated admin carry every project. Scheduling, compliance and deadlines are not an agent’s job here, which is why ours spend their time with buyers and nurturing leads instead of chasing signatures.

Every lead, from the first day

The sign goes up before the old house comes down, with your logo on it. Drive-bys, cold calls, agent enquiries, event guests — all of it lands in one place, and all of it gets the campaign. We do not lose somebody because they passed the site in the wrong month.

You are not the only one being marketed

Every technique email, every interview, every post carries Kaeser Building as much as it carries the address. We would rather sign the next three projects with you than win this one.
