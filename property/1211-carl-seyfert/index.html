The Residence

A Study in Presence and Detail

Tucked at the end of a quiet dead-end street in coveted Forest Hills, just off Granny White, this newly built 1.28-acre estate delivers the rare combination of privacy, scale, and finish that today's luxury buyer is searching for. Completion is set for mid-October. Designed with timeless curb appeal, the exterior pairs hand-laid stone with soft slurry-washed masonry beneath a steeply pitched roofline — a façade that will age beautifully for generations.

Inside, the home is a study in warm, livable luxury: white oak ceiling detail, stone accents in the foyer, oversized steel-look windows, and a layout built around the way families actually live. Six bedrooms and six full baths, plus two half baths, are anchored by a main-level primary suite and a generous secondary bedroom on the first floor — ideal for guests, in-laws, or multi-generational living. A dedicated main-floor office, an upstairs gym, and three laundry rooms (up, down, and inside the primary) round out a floor plan engineered for real life at the highest level.

The kitchen is built for both entertaining and everyday ease, with a full butler's pantry concealing a complete second kitchen behind cabinetry — a true working back-of-house that keeps the main space immaculate. Storage throughout the home is exceptional.

Step outside to a resort-caliber backyard: a full pool and spa — with an available retractable cover option — plus an outdoor kitchen, a covered living area with overhead heaters, and a dedicated pool bath, all framed by professional landscaping and lavender-lined hardscape. A four-car garage and pre-wiring for security cameras and whole-home audio (with speakers already installed in the living room, primary suite, and outdoor area) complete the package.
