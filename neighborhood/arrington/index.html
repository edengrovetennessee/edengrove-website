The Setting

Arrington is an unincorporated community in eastern Williamson County, settled originally as Petersburg and with a post office from 1858. It sits in rolling farmland roughly twenty-five miles south of Nashville, and is known as the heart of Tennessee's wine country. State Route 840 gives quick access to I-65 and I-24.

The Market

03

The Houses

Housing spans construction years from 1900 to the present, so historic farmhouses sit alongside large new construction in gated and acreage subdivisions such as Kings Chapel, Hardeman Springs and Black Hawk. Architecture leans traditional, with Craftsman, modern farmhouse and European influences most common.
