What It Costs to Hold

Property is assessed at a quarter of value, and Williamson cut its rate about 30% in 2025. A $3M house runs roughly $11,175 a year in Brentwood, against about $52,000 in Cook County, Illinois. Dues in the communities we cover run $3,000 to $5,700.

Two Markets, Twenty Minutes Apart

Williamson’s median asking price is $1,155,000 against Davidson’s $525,000, and the price per square foot runs $358 against $301. Where you look changes what your budget buys and how hard you can push.

New Construction

The Builder’s Contract Is Not the Standard One

A resale runs on the balanced Tennessee Realtors form. A builder transaction runs on the builder’s own. Read allowances, change-order markup, the completion date and what happens when it slips, escalation, deposit escrow and arbitration.

Allowances are where the money goes: a dollar line for flooring, lighting or appliances that you exceed at retail, and the most common reason a $2M contract closes well above $2M.

In Fairness

The Honest Caveat

A Private Exclusive has no public days on market and no price history, so you have less evidence that the ask is reasonable. That is the mirror of the seller’s advantage, and our job is to supply the evidence from the closing record.

Nor do you have to use us to see them — Compass opens its book to agents at any brokerage. We would rather tell you than have you find out later.

Representation, Money and Closing

What Representation Costs

Since 2024 a written agreement is required before we can show you a house. Read the term and the exit. You get the fee in writing before you tour.

Who Pays It

Usually still the seller, negotiated outside the MLS, because offering nothing shrinks their own buyer pool. If they offer less than the agreed fee, the difference is yours — written in as a concession or paid at closing.

Financing and Proof of Funds

Above $2M you are in jumbo regardless of the high-cost limit, and underwriting runs longer. Sellers expect verification before they engage; we will tell you what can be redacted.

No Attorney Required at Closing

Tennessee is a title company state and no attorney is required. On a builder contract we would still want your own counsel reading it.
